Reports

QuickFile Reporting

Ledger Line Reports turns a QuickFile backup into 58 reports across 6 categories. It covers the standard QuickFile statements, ledgers and sales and purchase reports, plus advanced reports QuickFile doesn't offer. Your backup is read in the browser tab and never uploaded.

  • 58 reports
  • Runs in your browser
  • Nothing uploaded
  • Free to use
Quick guide
  1. In QuickFile, download a backup of your account as a .zip file.
  2. Open the Reporting tool and choose that backup .zip.
  3. Pick a report, then set the date range and filters.
  4. Export the rows to CSV or copy them into Excel or Google Sheets.

Records

QuickFile Archive

Open your QuickFile records straight from a backup, even after the account is closed. Browse invoices, purchases, payments, contacts, bank statements and ledger postings, re-create invoices as PDFs, and check the Trial Balance, Profit and Loss and Balance Sheet at any date.

  • Invoices as PDFs
  • Read-only
  • Nothing uploaded
  • Keep 6 years of records
Quick guide
  1. Download a full backup .zip from QuickFile and keep it somewhere safe.
  2. Open the Archive Viewer and choose your backup .zip.
  3. Use the Show menu to switch between invoices, purchases, contacts, bank and reports.
  4. Click any row to see the full record, or download an invoice as a PDF.

Contact support

Contacting the Support Team

We don't offer telephone support. The quickest way to get help is through the Community Forum.

  • General questions: post them on the Community Forum. The support team monitors it every day, and other users can help too.
  • Sensitive details: if your question includes company names, account numbers or anything else private, send the support team a private message through the forum instead of posting publicly.

Please note: the Community Forum is hosted by a third party and doesn't use your QuickFile login. If you haven't already, you'll need to sign up for a separate forum account before you can post or send a private message. For your security, please don't reuse the password you use for QuickFile.
